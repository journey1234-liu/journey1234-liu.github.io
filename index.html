About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Medical Robotics (IMR), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Yun Gu. My research focuses on topology-aware medical image analysis — including segmentation and centerline tracking of vessel-like tubular structures, and foundation models for topological knowledge of vascular anatomy.

ATM26 Challenge Organizer. I am a member of the organizing team for the ATM26 challenge (Airway Tree Modeling for Endobronchial Surgery). See the challenge website and the public leaderboard.

Research Overview

"Building topology-aware models that explicitly understand connectivity and branching of vessel-like anatomy for reliable medical image analysis."

Topology-Aware Foundation Models

Developing multimodal foundation models that unify topological knowledge understanding and segmentation generation for vessel-like tubular anatomy (TubeMLLM).

Vessel Skeleton Tracking

Designing graph-based centerline tracking methods that preserve topological consistency for vessel modeling and navigation (TopoVST).
