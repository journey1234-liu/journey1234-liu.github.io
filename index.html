About Me

I am a student at the Institute of Medical Robotics (IMR), Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on medical image analysis, with current work on automatic airway-tree modeling from chest CT — including binary airway segmentation and branch-wise anatomical labeling.

Airway Tree Modeling. I work on the ATM26 challenge (Airway Tree Modeling 2026), spanning Track 1 (binary airway segmentation) and Track 2 (branch-wise anatomical labeling). See the public leaderboard.

Research Overview

"Building reliable, clinically deployable systems that connect medical visual perception with quantitative anatomical analysis."

Airway Segmentation

Developing methods to segment the airway tree from chest CT, addressing the challenge of thin, tubular structures and their topological correctness.

Anatomical Labeling

Assigning branch-wise anatomical labels (21 segmental classes) to segmented airways, evaluated on classification and voxel-level agreement.
